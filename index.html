About

The Ohio State Roller Hockey Club is a student-run club for those wishing to play roller hockey competitively during their time at The Ohio State University. We compete as a member of the Midwest Collegiate Roller Hockey League (MCRHL), a part of the National Collegiate Roller Hockey Association (NCRHA), against collegiate teams from across the region and the country. Currently, our club fields both a Division 2 team as well as a AA team. We welcome players regardless of experience levels, from those transitioning from ice hockey to those discovering roller hockey for the first time.

Currently, those in the club can expect to practice once a week, with dates and times varying on availability, at the recently opened Heatwave Arena. For our games, we typically travel around 3-4 weekends per semester to other locations around the region, including St. Louis, Detroit, Chicago, Indianapolis, and Pittsburgh. We will likely have 1-2 weekends take place at Heatwave Arena over the course of the season. We will also travel to Nationals if we qualify, which may be held anywhere in the US.

If you are interested in joining the club, please fill out the interest form on our website, and we will reach out to you with more information. We look forward to seeing you on the rink!
